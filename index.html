Questions

Good to know before you come

What ages is the kids area for?

Children aged 2 to 10.

Can I watch my child while I am in the café?

Yes. The café has live screens, and HD cameras monitor the play area.

How do I book play time?

At reception. You get a numbered ticket with your child's end time, and we let you know when it is nearly up.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, free high-speed Wi-Fi for every guest.

Do menu prices include VAT?

No. Prices are in Egyptian pounds and 14% VAT is added.

Come play, stay for coffee

Bring the kids for an hour or an afternoon. Our team at reception will set up their play time and your table.
